# Teacher quick-start (10 minutes)

## In class

- **Project a lab or diagram** from *Labs* while you teach – e.g. drag the light slider in the photosynthesis lab, or switch on crossing over in the meiosis stepper.
- **Diagram drills:** open a diagram in *Find it* or *Name it* mode and let learners call out answers – exactly how Section B diagram questions work.
- **Predict, then reveal:** in the Punnett lab, set up a cross, let learners predict the ratio on paper, then press *Reveal*.

## Homework and revision

- Assign a **lesson** (send the link). Learners can switch on *one step at a time* and *read aloud*.
- Use the **worksheet generator** (*Teachers → Worksheet generator*): choose topics and a length; the memo prints on a separate page. The same link always produces the same worksheet.
- Ask learners to do a **quick mock** (about 50 marks) and bring their topic breakdown to class.

## Exam preparation

- **Mock papers** follow the Grade 11 and 12 Paper 1 / Paper 2 split, 1 minute per mark, with standard, extra-time (+25%) and untimed options. Print the paper and the memo, or let learners self-mark online.
- The **weak-spot coach** (on the Progress page) turns quiz, drill, Exam Room and mock results into a mastery score per topic and suggests what to do next.

## Learners with barriers to learning

Show learners the **Preferences** panel once: dyslexia-friendly fonts, text size and spacing, a reading ruler, word anchors, calm mode, high contrast, focus mode and the focus-sprint timer. Diagrams can be explored one part at a time and every part can be read aloud. Mock papers have an extra-time option.

## Careers and everyday biology

Use the **Careers** lessons in Grade 11 subject-choice and career-guidance weeks, and the **APS calculator** before university applications. The **Everyday biology** lessons link directly to CAPS content (nutrition, immunity, human reproduction, ecology) and work well for Life Orientation integration.
