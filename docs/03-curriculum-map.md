# CAPS curriculum map

LifeMzansi covers the CAPS Life Sciences curriculum for **Grades 11 and 12** (Department of Basic Education). The weightings below follow the DBE examination guidelines approximately; they change a little each year and some provinces split the Grade 11 content between the papers differently – always check the current guidelines and your Annual Teaching Plan.

All papers: **150 marks, 2½ hours**. Section A (50 marks): multiple choice, terminology, matching and short data questions. Section B (100 marks): longer questions with data, diagrams, graphs and investigations.

## Scientific skills (both grades, both papers)

Planning a Scientific Investigation · Tables, Graphs & Data · Biological Drawings & Magnification – with the investigation planner, graph lab and microscope lab. Skills questions are dealt out across all four mock papers.
