# Overview

**LifeMzansi** is a free, interactive companion for **CAPS Grade 11 and 12 Life Sciences** – built for South African learners and teachers, and designed from the ground up for learners who learn differently (ADHD, dyslexia and other learning barriers).

It is the sister app of MathsMzansi, PhysicsMzansi, EnglishMzansi, AccountingMzansi, GeoMzansi and CodeMzansi, and shares their learning design and accessibility features.

## What's inside

| Area | What learners get |
|---|---|
| **Lessons** | Every CAPS Grade 11 and 12 topic, in short steps (2–14 minutes each), with objectives, key terms and the CAPS reference. |
| **Tappable diagrams** | 20 schematic diagrams – the eye, ear, brain, neuron, reflex arc, nephron, kidney, alveolus, digestive system, flower, leaf, chloroplast, mitochondrion, virus, bacterium, sperm, the male and female reproductive systems, the amniotic egg and DNA. Explore them part by part, then "find it" and "name it" like an exam question. |
| **Labs** | Punnett squares, DNA & protein synthesis, meiosis, photosynthesis limiting factors, respiration, mark–recapture and growth curves, natural selection, homeostasis, the menstrual cycle, the eye, an investigation planner, a graph lab and a microscope lab. |
| **Scientific skills** | Investigations, hypotheses and variables, reliability and validity, tables and graphs (the 6-mark rubric), biological drawings and magnification – examined in every paper. |
| **Practice** | Endless question generators (genetics crosses, DNA base pairing, meiosis numbers, population estimates, magnification and Section A terminology for every topic), lesson quizzes, practice problems with hints and full solutions. |
| **Exam Room & mock papers** | NSC-style questions with one-tick-per-mark memos for every examined topic, and timed Grade 11 and 12 Paper 1 and Paper 2 mocks with achievement levels and a topic breakdown. |
| **Careers** | Health, farming, nature, lab and biotech careers, a career explorer, an APS calculator and the routes through university, TVET and learnerships. |
| **Everyday biology** | Eating well on a budget, food labels, germs, vaccines and antibiotics, sexual and reproductive health, first aid (with a CPR rhythm trainer), the brain and substances, growing food and safe water. |
| **Revision** | Spaced-repetition flashcards, a glossary, the Quiz Arena, a weak-spot coach and seven revision games. |

## Principles

1. **Do, then explain.** Every idea comes with something to tap, drag, build or predict.
2. **One idea at a time.** Short chunks, worked examples revealed step by step, no walls of text.
3. **Exam-ready language.** Key terms, action verbs and memo-style answers – because spelling and terminology earn marks.
4. **Biology for life.** Careers and everyday biology show why the subject matters beyond matric.
5. **Private by default.** No accounts and no tracking; progress stays on the device.
