# Careers & everyday biology

Two areas go beyond the exam. They are not examined, but each lesson links back to the CAPS content it uses – so they double as revision.

## Careers (vocational learning)

| Lesson | What it covers |
|---|---|
| Careers in Health & Care | Nursing, medicine, pharmacy, emergency care, therapies, dietetics, radiography, community health work; registration bodies; job shadowing |
| Careers in Farming, Food & Nature | Farming, agricultural science, vets and animal health, food technology, conservation, field guiding, horticulture; green small businesses |
| Careers in the Lab, Biotech & Forensics | Medical technology, lab assistants, forensic science, biotechnology, research; lab safety |
| Your Path After Matric | APS, NSC pass levels, universities, universities of technology, TVET colleges and learnerships, NSFAS and bursaries, an application timeline, scam warnings |

**Tools:** a career explorer (interest quiz plus a searchable list with the study route to each career) and an APS calculator (saved only on the learner's device).

## Everyday biology

| Lesson | Links to CAPS |
|---|---|
| Eat Well, Spend Less | Animal nutrition, balanced diet, nutritional disorders |
| Germs, Vaccines & Medicines | Microorganisms, immunity, evolution in action (resistance) |
| Sexual & Reproductive Health | Human reproduction, the menstrual cycle, HIV |
| First Aid That Saves Lives | Gas exchange, circulation, the nervous system |
| Brain, Sleep, Stress & Substances | Nervous and endocrine systems, homeostasis |
| Grow Your Own Food | Photosynthesis, plant reproduction, food security, ecology |
| Safe Water & a Healthy Home | Human impact, microorganisms and disease |

**Tools:** a budget plate builder and food-label decoder, the germ lab (handwashing, herd immunity, antibiotics), a first-aid trainer with a CPR rhythm trainer, and the water and carbon footprint calculator.

## A note on sensitive topics

Sexual health, substances and mental health are taught factually and without judgement, in line with CAPS and Life Orientation. Learners are pointed to free, confidential services: public clinics, Childline (116), the GBV Command Centre (0800 428 428) and SADAG (0800 567 567). Check that numbers are still current before printing them for your class.
