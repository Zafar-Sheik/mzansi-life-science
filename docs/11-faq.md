# FAQ & troubleshooting

**Does it cover Grade 10?** No – LifeMzansi focuses on Grades 11 and 12 (the content examined in the NSC). Scientific skills, careers and everyday biology are useful from Grade 10.

**Our school puts a Grade 11 topic in a different paper.** Paper allocations for Grade 11 differ between provinces and years. The Exam Room lets you filter by topic, and the worksheet generator lets you choose any mix of topics.

**Why are the diagrams simplified?** They are schematic on purpose – one clear shape per structure – so learners can focus on one part at a time. Learners should also study the diagrams in their textbook and past papers.

**Read aloud doesn't work.** It needs a browser with speech synthesis (most do). On some phones you must install a voice in the system settings.

**Will progress move to a new phone?** Use Progress → Export, then Import on the new device.

**Are the career requirements and APS scores exact?** No – they are a guide. Requirements change every year and differ between institutions; always check the latest prospectus.

**How accurate is the content?** It follows CAPS and the DBE examination guidelines and is written to support, not replace, your teacher and textbook. Weightings change slightly each year – check the current guidelines.
