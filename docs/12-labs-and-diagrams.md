# Labs, diagrams & tools

All labs live on the **Labs** page (filter by grade and area) and inside the lessons that use them. Nothing moves until the learner presses a button, and each lab has an *Explain* box.

## Diagrams (explore · find it · name it)

Eye, ear, brain, neuron, reflex arc, nephron, kidney, alveolus, digestive system, flower, leaf, chloroplast, mitochondrion, virus, bacterium, sperm, female and male reproductive systems, amniotic egg and DNA.

- **Explore:** tap a part (or its name in the list) to see what it does; read it aloud.
- **Find it:** "Tap the cochlea."
- **Name it:** "What is part C?" with four options – the way Section B asks.
